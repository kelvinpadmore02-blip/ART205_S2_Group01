ABOUT THE CONCEPT

Skills for everyday opportunity.

Digital Training Center is a sample business concept for the ART205 Group 1 project, focused on practical and welcoming digital learning.

OUR PURPOSE

Make technology feel usable.

We imagine a local learning space where people can build confidence with computers, office tools, design and the web. Lessons move from a clear demonstration to guided practice and a small project.

This website is an educational demonstration. It does not represent a real institution or offer actual enrollment.
